Trace the sun through your actual windows.

Scan the room once (∼30 seconds, LiDAR iPhone) — Scrim rebuilds its walls, windows and doors, then shows where sunlight lands on the floor at any hour of the day.

Set north once with the dial, and the light pools are real: “the sun hits the sofa through the west window at 4:12.”
